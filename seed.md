I'd like to create a new game using the same engine as C:\Users\ninja\code\swords-and-arrows. Same swords, same arrows, same characters. The little men from swords and arrows are named sir tectus, sir bareti, sir fluctus, and sir morgani (i just came up with those names, no reference to them yet in swords and arrows).

Full controller support.

This will be a 1-4 player side scrolling metroidvania local co-op rogue like. There will be 12 biomes, 4 easy, 4 medium, and 4 hard. The maps will be procedurally generated. Difficulty scales on number of player playing, so it can be played solo or with 4 players.

The game is called Sir Tectus and the Golden Sandwich.

You will randomly play one of the easy biomes, and you must collect all the coins on the map to proceed to the next map. There will be 12 coins on each map, and you must collect all 12 coins to proceed to the next map.

Then you will proceed to the next randomly generated map, which could be any of the easy biomes again, or any of the medium biomes. Next time you must pick a medium or hard biome. Then you must pick a hard biome. Then you must pick the golden sandwich. So you must play 5 maps. At least 2 must be easy or medium, 1 medium and 1 hard. 

The goal is to collect the Golden Sandwich.

There will be 4 characters to play as. Each character will have a different ability. 

Sir Tectus can double jump and reflect projectiles with his shield and he's the only one who can use a shield. He can also throw his shield as a boomerang.
Sir Bareti can triple jump and shoot fire 
Sir Fluctus can air dash and create water slides to slide down slopes
Sir Morgani can double jump and throw swords

They all have the bow and arrow as a secondary attack.
